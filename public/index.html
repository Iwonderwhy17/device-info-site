      How to read the grade

      The grade counts only the details that identify you: the ones that look like your own hardware and software. A value that is the same for everyone, or that changes on each read, identifies less. Each letter corresponds to a range of identifying bits, and bits double with every step: about 1 in 16 at A, about 1 in 1,000 at B, and so on.

      The bit values come from a proposed model that has not yet been calibrated against real visitors. Treat the grade as an estimate, not a verdict. Two things matter more than the letter. First, the grade reflects what this page can read, not everything a tracker can see. Second, whether your browser can be linked across sites depends on tests this page does not yet run.

      No analytics, no third-party scripts, no fingerprint uploads, no database. The address and headers are returned to you and not stored.
